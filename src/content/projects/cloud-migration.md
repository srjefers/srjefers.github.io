models, orchestration patterns, and SLAs. Stack: Python, PySpark, Dagster, Snowflake,
dbt. Validated end-to-end with data-quality checks; **reduced cloud spend by 45%**
and, after optimizations identified during re-platforming, **improved pipeline
execution times by 35%**.
